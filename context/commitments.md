## Viability thesis
Who pays: Apify Store users and, since 2026, AI agents paying per request (x402, MPP, Skyfire).
For what: results from niche or rising-site actors that the first-party and large developers
do not cover. Order of magnitude: $1–5 per 1k results; a single niche actor at $0–300 a month,
80% of which reaches the developer after Apify's commission and before platform costs.
What has to be true for this to make money: a niche actor can be kept green by a machine at a
compute-plus-attention cost below its payout, and being early on a rising site is worth more
than the copy that arrives later.

## Kill criteria — pre-committed
Abandon or pivot if:
- No run of any actor has been paid for by someone other than the user by **2027-01-31**.

Checked at every `/status`. Tripping one is a decision point, not an automatic stop —
but the choice must be logged in `decisions.md` either way.
