## Context
- Zero actors, one operator at 6–8 hours a week, kill date 2027-01-31. The Kun pass on the
  plan found three durable loops built before any event existed for them; the pass on the
  architecture found the same pattern one level down.
- The repair workflow (C5) carries the widest-blast-radius secret and the subscription terms
  risk (ADR 001) before a single escalation exists to repair. The brief's "done" is satisfied
  by "repaired a real break **or** escalated one with a named cause".
- The builder is serial; the first approved candidate ends the search for about four weeks. A
  weekly routine producing five eleven-section notes meanwhile is twenty notes nobody can act
  on, each costing the operator a quarter of an hour.
- Milestone 1b required a graded `store-gaps.md` entry, but the only writer of synthesis
  pages was the synthesis routine of milestone 1d. Nothing wrote it in 1b.
- `notify.yml` was a second Telegram holder for a weekly ping the daily check could carry.
- "Alive versus dead" was a log gap noticed by a weekly summary sent over the channel most
  likely to be the dead part.

## Decision
1. **C5 waits for the first real escalation.** Until then `uv run foundry repair <actor>` runs
   the same brief in the user's local Claude Code session — no repo secret, no workflow. The
   failure-class taxonomy the trust ladder needs is written from those first repairs. ADR 001's
   end state stands; only its timing moves.
2. **Target research runs on demand, capped at two notes per run, until actor #1 has
   `status: approved`**; weekly with a cap of five after that.
3. **`store-gaps.md`'s ranked table is generated by the weekly pull** from snapshot fields —
   `notice`, failed-and-timed-out share, rating below the category median, users falling across
   snapshots — with no model. The prose grade ("why the gap is real") waits for synthesis.
4. **One notifier.** `notify.yml` is not built. The daily check sends the candidate ping (new
   candidate PRs since its last run) and, on Mondays, the weekly summary.
5. **A status page and a loud exit.** Every check regenerates `maintainer/STATUS.md` — per
   actor: last check, L1/L2, paused since, consecutive log days, exit-12 count in 24 h; plus
   last snapshot date, candidate PRs awaiting a merge, approved candidates awaiting their
   `decisions.md` line, fixtures awaiting approval — and exits **non-zero** whenever any of
   those needs a human, so GitHub's own failure e-mail is the alarm. The cron runs off the top
   of the hour (GitHub names the hour mark as its drop window). The workflow badge sits in
   `README.md`.

## Alternatives
- **Build everything in ADRs 001 and 007 now.** Rejected: attention spent on loops with no
  input, before the one loop that matters has run for seven days.
- **Drop target research until actor #1 exists.** Rejected: 1c is how actor #1 gets chosen.
- **Keep `notify.yml`.** Rejected: a second secret holder for at most a day of latency.
- **Alive/dead from the weekly summary alone.** Rejected: the summary travels over the channel
  under test.

## Consequences
- Milestone 1 proves check, pause, escalate and status — not repair automation; the "machine
  repairs" half of the thesis is tested after a real break exists, a few weeks later.
- `foundry repair` is one more CLI subcommand; `maintainer/STATUS.md` is one more generated
  file (never hand-edited); the pull grows a `store-gaps` generator.
- Plan rows 1, 1b, 1c and the risk table change; `CLAUDE.md` names the local repair route.
