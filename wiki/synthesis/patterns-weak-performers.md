# Patterns — what weak-performing actors share

Consolidated map, re-synthesised at each monthly run. Every claim links to the actor pages
under `../evidence/actors/` that evidence it; a claim with no link is removed at the next synthesis.
"Weak" is measured, not claimed: failed or aborted runs dominating the 30-day counts,
users flat or falling across snapshots, rating below the category median, `UNDER_MAINTENANCE`
or `RESIDENTIAL_PROXY_REQUIRED` notices, reviews saying it stopped working.

Compare within a category, never across the whole Store, and grade each claim observed,
documented or confirmed (see the wiki README). An observed pattern is a
hypothesis until one of our own actors confirms it: many top actors are top because they
were first, and many weak ones are weak for reasons the Store data cannot show.
