# Retention and feedback

Re-synthesised at each monthly run by the wiki research agent. Every claim carries an
evidence grade (observed, documented or confirmed — see the wiki README) and links its
evidence; a claim with no link is removed at the next synthesis.

For other people's actors this is inferred from user trends across snapshots — the Store API does not expose retention. Real retention, funnel and paid-user numbers come only from our own actors (`../first-party/`).
