# Store gaps — ranked Store-side gaps

Re-ranked at each monthly synthesis. Store-side evidence only: where the Store itself shows a
buyer badly served. One input to the target research agent, which checks the site, scores it
and writes the full candidate note (see [[candidate-card]]). An entry here is never a build
trigger on its own.

Each entry carries:
- the query or data a buyer plausibly wants;
- the best existing actor for it, linked to its page under `../evidence/actors/`, with its
  30-day success rate, rating, review count, notice and price;
- why the gap is real — no actor, a failing actor, a proxy-walled actor, or one under
  maintenance — graded per the evidence grades in the wiki README.
