# Candidate rulings log

Append-only, oldest first. One row per ruling on a candidate note. It measures how often the
target research agent's call matches yours: the trust ladder (telos R5) allows automating any
part of candidate review only after 10 consecutive matches, and the scoring weights in
[[candidate-card]] change only on evidence from this table. Publishing stays a human ruling
regardless.

The four sub-scores (demand, gap, growth, fetch) are recorded separately so the component that
mispredicted can be named. "Match" means the agent's ranking would have produced your status
without your edits: yes, or no with the reason.
